Minerals Technologies Inc. (MTI) generated $286.5 million in operating income and $167.1 million in net income in 2024, with a 13.5% operating margin and 25.9% production margin — indicating strong, sustained profitability [CITE:TARGET_M0002] [CITE:TARGET_M0003] [CITE:TARGET_M0022] [CITE:TARGET_M0019].

In comparison:
- Global Gas Corp reported an operating loss of $551.98 million and a net loss of $130.7 million in 2024, reflecting unprofitable operations [CITE:PEER_M0023] [CITE:PEER_M0011].
- Arcadium Lithium plc reported an operating loss of $53.5 million and a net loss of $78.9 million in 2024, despite $288.6 million in gross profit [CITE:PEER_M0092] [CITE:PEER_M0094].
- NL Industries Inc. reported $37.9 million in operating income and $59.3 million in net income (calculated from $83.3 million pre-tax income minus $14.1 million tax expense), significantly lower than MTI’s profitability [CITE:PEER_M0053] [CITE:PEER_M0046].

MTI’s profitability significantly exceeds all peers. Global Gas Corp and Arcadium Lithium are unprofitable, while NL Industries is profitable but at a fraction of MTI’s scale and margin. MTI is clearly the most profitable among the peers reviewed.
