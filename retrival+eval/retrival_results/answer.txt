SolarWinds Corporation faces elevated risks primarily centered on its substantial indebtedness, cybersecurity vulnerabilities, and uncertainty surrounding a pending merger. Its total debt as of 2024 was $1.2 billion, with a first lien net leverage ratio of 4.75 to 1.00, indicating high financial leverage that limits flexibility and increases vulnerability to economic downturns [CITE:TARGET_R0196][CITE:TARGET_R0207][CITE:TARGET_M0018]. The company also faces significant cybersecurity risks, including exposure to supply-chain attacks, legacy product vulnerabilities, and ongoing SEC litigation related to a prior cyber incident [CITE:TARGET_R0032][CITE:TARGET_R0038][CITE:TARGET_R0054]. Additionally, the pending merger introduces material uncertainty, including the risk of termination, a $119.2 million termination fee if SolarWinds terminates the agreement, and potential disruption to operations and customer relationships [CITE:TARGET_R0001][CITE:TARGET_R0022][CITE:TARGET_R0027].

In comparison, peers exhibit different risk profiles. SpringBig Holdings, Inc. faces risks tied to its unprofitable business model, with a $1.6 million net loss in 2024 and a $1.5 million working capital deficiency, alongside regulatory exposure from its association with the cannabis industry [CITE:PEER_R0012][CITE:PEER_R0013][CITE:PEER_R0015]. Nixxy, Inc. is unprofitable with a $22.6 million net loss in 2024 and has substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern due to negative operating cash flows and insufficient liquidity [CITE:PEER_R0205][CITE:PEER_R0204]. Cycurion, Inc. reported net income of $1.2 million in 2024 but carries a $7.8 million net working capital deficit and has substantial doubt about its going concern status due to negative operating cash flows and limited capital resources [CITE:PEER_R0268][CITE:PEER_R0271].

Unlike SolarWinds, none of the peers report comparable levels of indebtedness or merger-related risks. However, all peers face liquidity and profitability challenges, while SolarWinds’ primary risks are structural—debt burden, cybersecurity exposure, and transactional uncertainty—rather than operational solvency. SolarWinds’ profitability is strong, with $208.4 million GAAP operating income and $111.9 million net income in 2024, contrasting sharply with the peers’ losses [CITE:TARGET_M0009][CITE:TARGET_M0011].
