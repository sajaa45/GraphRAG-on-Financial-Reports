Minerals Technologies Inc. (MTI) generated a net income of $167.1 million in 2024 [CITE:TARGET_M0024]. Compared to peers, this exceeds VALHI INC’s net income of $108 million [CITE:PEER_M0070] and NL INDUSTRIES INC’s $67.2 million [CITE:PEER_M0156]. However, Global Gas Corp reported a net loss of $130.7 million in 2024 [CITE:PEER_M0005], making MTI the most profitable among the four.

MTI’s operating margin of 13.5% [CITE:TARGET_M0013] is lower than VALHI’s 21.1% [CITE:PEER_M0069] but higher than NL’s 15.5% (calculated from $83.3M income on $537.5M sales, inferred from peer data) and far superior to Global Gas Corp’s negative margin. VALHI’s higher margin reflects stronger pricing power despite cyclical TiO₂ exposure [CITE:PEER_R0099].

MTI faces pricing pressure risk from raw material cost increases it may not fully pass through [CITE:TARGET_R0004], but its profitability remains robust versus peers. Global Gas Corp is not a viable peer due to its unprofitable, pre-revenue status [CITE:PEER_R0001][CITE:PEER_R0003][CITE:PEER_R0006]. Among established peers, VALHI has a higher margin, but MTI delivers the highest absolute profit.
